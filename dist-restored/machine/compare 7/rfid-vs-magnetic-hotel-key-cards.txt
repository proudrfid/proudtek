## FAQ
- Q: When should a hotel keep magstripe instead of switching immediately?
  A: Keeping magstripe can still make sense when the installed estate is deeply tied to it and the upgrade case is not yet strong enough to justify broader change.
- Q: Does RFID only matter for room access?
  A: No. RFID becomes more valuable when the property wants to connect room access with amenity logic, faster reissue or a more modern guest experience.
