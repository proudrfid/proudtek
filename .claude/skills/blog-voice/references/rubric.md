# Scoring rubric — humor & enjoyable

Two independent 0–10 axes. They are not the same thing: a post can be unfunny
but very readable, or vice-versa. Score them separately, cite evidence (quote
the line), and check against the Step 5 acceptance bar.

## Humor (0–10) — "is the writer actively trying to amuse me?"

| Dimension | 0 looks like | 10 looks like |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| Deliberate beats | no jokes at all | designed bits, callbacks |
| Wit / irony | flat exposition | crisp dry wit, clean irony |
| Playful register | corporate-neutral | the writer is clearly enjoying it |
| Surprise / timing | fully predictable | comedic turn you didn't see coming |

This workflow targets **dry professional wit**, so the ceiling that matters is
~5–6: a few deliberate, on-tone beats on harmless targets. A 9–10 (laugh-out-loud)
is *off-spec* for B2B procurement — do not chase it.

## Enjoyable (0–10) — "do I want to keep reading?"

| Dimension | low | high |
|-----------|-----|------|
| Narrative pull | a list of facts | a hook that makes you read on |
| Voice / humanity | no "person" present | a real voice, a scene, an opinion |
| Concreteness | vague adjectives | numbers, brands, vivid detail |
| Payoff | nothing learned | a counter-intuitive "aha" |
| Skimmability | dense wall of bullets | scannable, conclusion-first |

## Acceptance bands

- **Tier A:** enjoyable ≥ 8, humor 5–6.
- **Tier B:** enjoyable ≥ 8, humor ≤ 2 (readability lift only; no jokes on the
  harm outcome).
- Both tiers: facts diff = 0, and all lint/check/snapshot gates green.

## Calibration anchor (use to keep scores consistent across sessions)

`rfid-card-demagnetized-myth-explained.json` (Tier A), the pilot:

- **Before — humor 2 / enjoyable 6.5.** Only involuntary dry wit ("the
  most-feared scenarios are the least-supported by physics"); opened on a
  definitional bullet; dense procurement tail.
- **After — humor 5 / enjoyable 8.** Three deliberate, on-tone beats
  ("the slightly awkward truth…", a cold-open scene where guest + front desk +
  search results "all confidently agree on an explanation that happens to be
  physically impossible", "cards conscripted into bottle-opener … duty"); the
  cold open replaced the definitional opening; **every spec sentence
  (25 µm bond wire, 70/75–80/100/200 °C, $0.30–0.60) unchanged.**
- The remaining 0.5 to 8.5 sits in move D (the dense SOP tail) — a good
  reminder that the cold open + voice do most of the enjoyable lift, and that
  leaving a strong existing closer alone (skipping move C) was the right call.
