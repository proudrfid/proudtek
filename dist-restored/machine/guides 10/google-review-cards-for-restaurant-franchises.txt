## FAQ
- Q: Do restaurant franchises need different review cards for every store?
  A: Not always. Some networks can share one design with editable routing, but others need location-specific versions because ownership, layout or review destinations differ too much.
- Q: What should a restaurant franchise pilot prove first?
  A: It should prove the routing logic, store-format fit, staff behavior and replenishment model before the brand commits to a larger cross-network print run.
