## Summary
- Laundry RFID projects depend less on generic tag pricing and more on whether the tag survives the wash profile, fits the textile workflow and reads reliably at the points where linen or garments actually move.
- Use these short answers to decide whether this page matches the project before moving into the detail.
- Start with wash profile, attachment method and reader setup.

## FAQ
- Q: Should laundry projects start with PPS or silicone tags?
  A: They should start with the wash profile and textile handling method. PPS often suits harsher hard-shell use, while silicone is often better where flexibility matters.
- Q: What makes laundry sample testing useful?
  A: Testing only becomes meaningful when the sample split reflects the real attachment method, textile type and wash-cycle stress.
- Q: Can hotels and industrial laundries use the same tag?
  A: Sometimes, but not by default. Similar frequencies do not guarantee the same durability or textile fit across very different laundry workflows.
