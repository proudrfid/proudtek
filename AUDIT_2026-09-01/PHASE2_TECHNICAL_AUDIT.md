## B. GEO-specific observations (extractability, verifiability, attribution)
- Extractable: every editorial page has a "Quick answer" block, spec tables and FAQ in `<details>`; JSON-LD `citation` arrays exist on articles; `/machine/*.json|txt` mirrors and `llms.txt` exist. **Good scaffolding.**
- Verifiable: the scaffolding repeats unverified first-party claims (T11) and cites official sources for some chip facts (`nxp.com`, `gs1.org` links found in content — counted in Phase 6). Verification status is the Phase 4 deliverable.
- Attributable: author identity inconsistent across surfaces (T9).
- Discoverable by AI crawlers: `robots.txt` allows them, **but the Vercel challenge (T2) may block them** — the single largest GEO risk found.

## C. Items verified clean (no action)
robots.txt syntax; sitemap XML validity (524/519 URLs all resolve); no redirect loops; www/https/trailing-slash consistency; no client-side-only content; no site JS errors; H1 uniqueness; canonical integrity; noindex scope; parameter canonicalisation; 404 status code; PSI a11y/SEO/BP 100 on homepage.

## D. Expected impact (interpretation)
T1–T4 are prerequisites: they change how much of the site Google fetches per day. T5–T7 protect legacy ranking signals and SERP presentation once pages are indexed. T8–T11 are the technical half of the credibility work whose content half is Phase 4. None of these guarantees rankings or citations.
