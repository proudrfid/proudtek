## FAQ
- Q: Can NFC door locks work without internet connectivity?
  A: Yes. Offline NFC locks are specifically designed for environments without network connectivity at the door. All authorization data is encoded on the card and validated locally by the lock. This architecture is standard in hotels, cruise ships and remote facilities.
- Q: What RFID chip should I specify for high-security door locks?
  A: MIFARE DESFire EV2 or EV3 with AES-128 mutual authentication and diversified keys. Avoid MIFARE Classic (known vulnerabilities) and 125 kHz proximity cards (no authentication). For the highest security, consider Java Card-based credentials with PKI certificate authentication.
- Q: How long do batteries last in NFC door locks?
  A: Offline NFC locks running on 4 AA batteries typically last 2–4 years or 30 000–50 000 openings. Online locks with Wi-Fi or BLE radios consume more power and last 6–18 months. Low-battery indicators warn maintenance staff 2–4 weeks before replacement is needed.
