- Title is 76 chars — above the snippet threshold. The exact-match `125 kHz Vs 13.56 MHz RFID` is front-loaded; the `LF Vs HF Frequency Comparison For Access Control` tail is keyword-rich but long. Suggested tighter form: "125 kHz vs 13.56 MHz RFID — Access Control Frequency Guide" (58 chars). Deferred to the compare-pillar title-polish pass.
- Summary is answer-first. Names both frequencies, names the key protocols (EM4100, HID Prox, ISO 14443, ISO 15693), and frames the decision as a security-vs-install-base trade-off. This is the paragraph an LLM will quote for "should I migrate from 125 kHz to 13.56 MHz".
- GEO hooks: the "Security reality — cloning attack economics" section is structured as cost-of-attack bullets ("Proxmark3 clone kit: under $400", "DEFCON demos since ~2008", etc.) which is the form an LLM uses for security-assessment answers — concrete costs rather than vague "less secure".
