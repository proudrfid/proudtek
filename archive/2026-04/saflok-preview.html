Frequently Asked Questions

Which MIFARE chip works with all Saflok lock series?
MIFARE Ultralight C is the only chip family that works across every current Saflok series — Quantum IV, Quantum Pixel, MT RFID, RT Plus, Confidant RFID and Confidant LED — without firmware changes. Classic 1K also works on all series but is limited to read-only mode on newer Quantum and MT locks. MIFARE Plus requires Ambiance v2.10 or later and is not supported on Confidant hardware.
Can I use MIFARE DESFire EV3 with Saflok Quantum IV locks?
No. Saflok locks do not support the DESFire chip family in any current firmware version. The Quantum IV accepts MIFARE Ultralight C and MIFARE Plus as its primary RFID credentials. Properties that need DESFire-level AES-128 encryption should evaluate MIFARE Plus EV2, which offers mutual authentication under ISO/IEC 14443A and is supported on Quantum IV with Ambiance v2.10 or later.
How do I find my Saflok lock firmware version before ordering cards?
The firmware version is displayed on the lock diagnostic screen when you hold a programming card to the reader, or it can be retrieved from the Ambiance software under Device Management. For System 6000 sites, the encoder utility reports the lock firmware during a status query. Providing this version number when requesting a test kit ensures ProudTek encodes sample cards against your exact configuration.
What happens if I install the wrong chip type in a Saflok lock?
The lock will reject the card at the reader with no audible or visual feedback beyond a red LED flash. Front-desk staff will not see an error in Ambiance — the card simply fails to open the door. In a batch-encoding scenario, this means hundreds of cards may be encoded successfully at the front desk but fail at every lock, creating a guest lockout situation that requires emergency re-ordering on a 2-3 day expedited timeline.
Does ProudTek offer pre-encoded Saflok-compatible cards?
ProudTek ships cards with the correct chip and antenna configuration but does not pre-encode guest-room credentials. Room-key encoding must happen at the property through Ambiance or System 6000 because it requires real-time PMS data such as room number, check-in date and access level. ProudTek can pre-encode common-area access credentials if the property provides the access-group configuration file.
What is the minimum order quantity for Saflok-compatible cards?
Plain white MIFARE Ultralight C cards start at 200 pieces per order. Custom-printed cards with hotel branding require a 500-piece minimum to cover the offset printing setup cost. Test-card kits of 5 pieces are available free of charge for new compatibility verification projects. Volume pricing tiers start at 5,000 and 10,000 pieces with per-card cost reductions of 12-18%.
Are Saflok-compatible cards affected by the Unsaflok vulnerability?
The Unsaflok vulnerability disclosed in 2024 affects MIFARE Classic 1K credentials on older Saflok firmware. dormakaba released firmware patches and recommends migrating to MIFARE Ultralight C. ProudTek can supply replacement Ultralight C cards as a drop-in upgrade for properties completing the firmware patch. The migration does not require lock hardware changes — only a firmware update and new card stock.
How long does a full Saflok compatibility verification take?
The typical timeline from initial inquiry to production approval is 10-14 business days. Stage 1 lock audit takes 1-2 days, test-card shipment takes 3 days, on-site validation takes 2-3 days and artwork approval adds 3-5 days for custom-printed orders. Properties ordering plain white stock can move from test-kit approval to bulk delivery in as few as 10 business days total.
Can Saflok-compatible cards include a magnetic stripe?
Yes. ProudTek produces dual-interface cards with an ISO/IEC 14443A RFID chip and an ISO 7811 HiCo magnetic stripe on the same card body. Some Saflok estates use the magstripe for elevator or parking access while the RFID chip handles room locks. The combo card costs approximately 15-20% more than an RFID-only card at the 5,000-piece tier.
