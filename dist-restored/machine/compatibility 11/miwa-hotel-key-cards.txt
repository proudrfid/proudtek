## FAQ
- Q: What helps a MIWA compatibility request move fastest?
  A: The fastest requests usually include a current guest card sample or a clear MIWA lock or encoder reference together with notes on printing, encoding and whether the property needs plain or premium cards.
- Q: Should MIWA projects start with premium materials?
  A: Usually no. It is safer to confirm the baseline compatible card path first, then add eco or premium material options once the property already knows the technical fit works.
