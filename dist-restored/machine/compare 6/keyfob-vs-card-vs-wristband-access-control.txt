## FAQ
- Q: Is there one best credential format for all access-control projects?
  A: No. The right format depends on how users actually carry the credential, how often it gets replaced and what the operating environment demands.
- Q: When is a mixed credential program useful?
  A: It can make sense when employees, residents, visitors or amenity users clearly benefit from different formats instead of a single compromise option.
