## Description
EM4200 125 kHz RFID cards for simple read-only proximity credentials, suited to access control, attendance and low-cost LF card issuance.

## Summary
- EM4200 cards are 125 kHz read-only RFID credentials for simple access control and attendance use cases where low cost, stable UID behavior and broad LF reader compatibility matter.
- The EM4200 card is an improved model on EM4100(discontinue), its most significant feature is the longer reading ranges than the old models.
- Although the EM-ID is the most simple RFID product, but is is widely deployed in the early years, it still has very wide application. EM4200 compatible with all these already deployed system.
