NFC Business Cards

Custom NFC business cards built around NTAG chip options for tap-to-share profiles, lead capture and digital contact exchange on iPhone and Android.

Categories: Products, RFID cards

Features

1, Seamless Digital Integration

Embedded with NFC chip, these business cards allow recipients to access your digital contact information, social profiles, promotional video, and websites with a simple tap of their smartphone.

2, Very thin Design

It can be as thin as 0.38mm, even thinner than the regular paper business card.

3, Can be Transparent

The chip position can be tranparent, which makes the business card looks High Tech visually.

4, Various Craft to Impress People

Various high end craft, like silver/gold metallic effect to make the card very top end at the first glance.

Technical Specifications

Chip OptionsNTAG213 (144 bytes), NTAG215 (504 bytes), NTAG216 (888 bytes)
Operating Frequency13.56 MHz (HF)
ProtocolISO 14443A, NFC Forum Type 2 Tag
Pre-Programmed DataNDEF URL record to digital profile (vCard, Popl, Linktree, custom landing page)
Material OptionsPVC, metal (stainless steel/brass), bamboo, walnut, recycled plastic
Dimensions85.6 × 54 mm (CR80) or custom die-cut shapes
PersonalizationLaser engraving, UV printing, foil stamping, QR code backup
Read RangePVC: 2–4 cm; Metal: 1–3 cm; Wood: 2–4 cm
Compatibility: Works with all NFC-enabled smartphones (iPhone XS and later, Android 5.0+); no app required for URL/vCard NDEF.

Applications

  • Professional networking and contact sharing
  • Real estate agent and sales team cards
  • Executive and C-suite premium cards

Buyer Notes

  1. 1NTAG213 is sufficient for a single URL redirect; NTAG216 for full vCard with photo, multiple social links, and fallback URL.
  2. 2Metal cards require ferrite shielding — verify NFC tap zone is clearly marked for consistent read performance.
  3. 3Include a printed QR code as fallback for phones with NFC disabled or older devices without NFC.
  4. 4URL-based profiles (Popl, Linktree, HiHello) allow updating contact info without reprogramming the card.
