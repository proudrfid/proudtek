## FAQ
- Q: What works best at a front desk: a card or a fixed prompt?
  A: That depends on how staff interacts with customers. A delivered card often works better in guided service moments, while a fixed prompt can suit desks where staff cannot reliably add another handoff step.
- Q: What should a front-desk pilot measure first?
  A: It should measure staff willingness to prompt, customer phone behavior, queue pressure and whether the review action fits naturally into the reception workflow.
