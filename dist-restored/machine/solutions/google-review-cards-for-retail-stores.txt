## FAQ
- Q: What usually works best for retail review prompts?
  A: The best format depends on how quickly the shopper moves through the counter and whether staff can naturally point to the prompt at the end of the purchase.
- Q: Should retail teams start with stickers or review cards?
  A: They should start with the format that matches the checkout behavior best, and many teams learn fastest by testing one card and one sticker in live store conditions.
