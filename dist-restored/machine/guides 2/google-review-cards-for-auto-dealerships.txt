## Summary
- This guide helps auto dealerships deploy Google review cards across sales and service locations by focusing on branch routing, handoff timing and whether the prompt belongs at the desk, delivery point or service pickup counter.
- Use these short answers to decide whether this page matches the project before moving into the detail.
- Dealership review prompts often split between sales delivery and service handoff moments.

## FAQ
- Q: Should dealerships use one review-card setup for sales and service?
  A: Not always. Sales delivery and service pickup often create different handoff moments, so the routing and physical format may need to differ even within one store.
- Q: What should a dealership pilot prove first?
  A: It should prove branch routing, department-level placement and whether the handoff actually fits sales and service workflow before a broader group rollout begins.
