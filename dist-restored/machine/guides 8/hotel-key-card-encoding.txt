## Summary
- This guide helps hotel teams scope card encoding work, reduce avoidable compatibility mistakes and prepare a tighter brief before asking for encoded samples or production support.
- Use these short answers to decide whether this page matches the project before moving into the detail.
- Encoding should be planned together with compatibility, not after the card material is chosen.

## FAQ
- Q: What should a hotel send before asking for pre-encoded card samples?
  A: The best first brief includes the lock or encoder environment, likely chip family, numbering rules, artwork needs and the pilot quantity and deadline.
- Q: What should an encoding pilot validate besides lock opening?
  A: It should also validate front-desk issue logic, replacement handling, staff workflow and whether the encoded card path is practical enough to scale.
