## FAQ
- Q: What should be set up first on a Google review NFC card?
  A: The first setup priority is the live review destination and who controls it, because every later design, encoding and rollout choice depends on that link working reliably.
- Q: Why is QR fallback still important on an NFC review card?
  A: QR fallback keeps the review prompt usable when a customer does not tap correctly, NFC is disabled or the phone behavior is inconsistent enough to need another path.
