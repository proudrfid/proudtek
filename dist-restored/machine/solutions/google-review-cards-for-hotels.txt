  A: They usually work best at checkout, concierge or other service moments where staff already interacts with guests and can naturally invite a review.
- Q: Should hotels use the same review prompt format everywhere?
  A: Not always. Some hotels do better with a desk-delivered card, while others benefit more from a static QR or sticker prompt in a specific guest area.
