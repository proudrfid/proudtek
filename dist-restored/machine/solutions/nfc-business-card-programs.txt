## FAQ
- Q: Should NFC business card projects choose material before chip?
  A: No. Mobile compatibility and the digital workflow should be confirmed first, then the material can be chosen around brand and production goals.
- Q: Are metal or wood cards always better than PVC?
  A: Only when the brand benefit justifies the cost, finish and production tradeoffs. Standard PVC still fits many broad team rollouts.
- Q: What information makes NFC card sampling faster?
  A: Target phones, redirect requirements, material preference and the size of the initial rollout are usually the most useful early inputs.
