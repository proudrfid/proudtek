## Summary
- Hotels rarely choose between cards and wristbands on aesthetics alone. The better decision usually comes from guest flow, property type, amenity access and how many credentials the staff can realistically issue and recover.
- Use these short answers to decide whether this page matches the project before moving into the detail.
- Hotel key cards - Traditional room access - Easy to issue, familiar for guests, scalable across properties

## FAQ
- Q: Are wristbands replacing hotel cards everywhere?
  A: No. Wristbands are gaining ground in resort and amenity-heavy contexts, but cards remain the default in many standard room-access programs.
- Q: When is a mixed program worth it?
  A: When the property has clearly different room and amenity workflows and can manage a more deliberate issuance process.
