## Summary
- The difference between on-metal NFC labels and standard NFC stickers is not cosmetic. It is a deployment question about surface, performance and whether the label has to work in an environment that breaks a normal sticker's read behavior.
- Use these short answers to decide whether this page matches the project before moving into the detail.
- Standard NFC sticker - Packaging, smart labels and non-metal surfaces - Simple, flexible and efficient for many campaigns

## FAQ
- Q: Can a standard NFC sticker be tested on metal anyway?
  A: It can be tested, but that often wastes time if the deployment is clearly metal-bound and already points to an on-metal path.
- Q: Does on-metal always mean a harder, thicker label?
  A: Not always, but it usually means the label construction must account for the metal environment rather than assuming a normal sticker will behave the same.
