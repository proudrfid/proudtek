## RFID laundry tag wash-cycle ratings (ruled 2026-07-11)

| Product | Rated industrial wash cycles |
| --- | --- |
| Textile / woven RFID laundry tag | **200-300** |
| Silicone RFID laundry tag | **50-100** |
| PPS RFID laundry tag | **300-500** |

Application rules that have already caught real mistakes:

- **Attribute the number to the material, never to "laundry tags"
  generically.** A generic claim must either name the span with its ends
  attributed ("50-500 by construction — silicone 50-100, textile 200-300,
  PPS 300-500") or pick the material the sentence is actually about.
- **Tag life ≠ garment life.** "Rental uniforms last 150-200 wash cycles"
  is a garment fact; "linen rated for 50-100 washes" is a linen fact.
  Don't "correct" those to tag numbers — the 2026-07-11 site-wide audit
  deliberately left them alone.
- **Third-party test results and observed case-study values stay as
  reported** (e.g. RFIDHY's published 520-cycle ISO/IEC 18046-3 test, a
  case study's "250+ cycles observed across 1.8M garments"). Ratings are
  ours; observations are theirs/history.
- **Temperatures were not part of the ruling** — leave existing °C claims
  unless they contradict a source.
- History: the pre-2026-07-11 site mixed these numbers freely (the textile
  page carried the silicone number for months, and one pass even
  "corrected" the right value to the wrong one by majority vote). Majority
  vote across pages is NOT evidence for company facts — this table is.
