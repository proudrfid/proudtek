## FAQ
- Q: What should decide the first RFID reader test purchase?
  A: The first test purchase should be driven by the exact credential family, host environment and workflow the team wants to validate rather than by generic feature lists.
- Q: When does a reader shortlist go wrong most often?
  A: It usually goes wrong when teams buy hardware before confirming protocol fit, SDK needs and whether the reader is for testing, issuance or live operational use.
