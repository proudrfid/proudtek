| Separate brands? | RFIDAK is positioned as a separate brand of the same company (owner-attested). "Proud RFID" / "PROTEK RFID" / "Proudtek" are spelling variants, not brands. | owner-attested |
| Distributors / unrelated? | No evidence any of the five is a distributor or unrelated; all use the company's own contact channels. | — |

Conclusion: Google and answer engines currently see **one address, one phone number, one founding year and one customer base attached to at least three legal-name spellings, three sets of headline facts and five domains**. This is the entity-consistency problem.

## 5. Interpretation and expected impact
Fixing entity consistency does not by itself move rankings. It removes the reasons an answer engine or a buyer would distrust the site: contradictory capability statements (C2) and unverifiable certification claims (C3) are the two items that can turn from "SEO issue" into a factual-accuracy liability. Everything else (NAP, sameAs hygiene, logo, spelling) is low-effort once C1–C3 are decided.
