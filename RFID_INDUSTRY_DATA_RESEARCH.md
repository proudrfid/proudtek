## 3. Technical Specifications Data

### Read Range Comparison: LF vs HF vs UHF

| Parameter | LF (125/134 kHz) | HF (13.56 MHz) | UHF (860-960 MHz) |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Frequency** | 125 kHz / 134.2 kHz | 13.56 MHz | 860-960 MHz |
| **Typical Read Range** | 1-10 cm | 1-100 cm (NFC: 1-5 cm) | 1-15 meters |
| **Data Transfer Rate** | Low (~8 kbps) | Medium (~106-424 kbps) | High (~40-640 kbps) |
| **Anti-Collision** | Limited / None | Yes (ISO 14443/15693) | Yes (EPC Gen2) |
| **Tag Cost** | $0.10-$2.00 | $0.10-$5.00 | $0.05-$0.50 |
| **Metal/Liquid Performance** | Good near metal | Moderate | Poor (without special tags) |
| **Typical Applications** | Access control, animal ID | Payments, transit, hotel keys | Retail, logistics, inventory |
| **Key Standards** | ISO 11784/85, EM4100 | ISO 14443, ISO 15693, NFC | ISO 18000-6C, EPC Gen2v2 |
| **Power Source** | Passive | Passive | Passive or Active |
| **Multi-Tag Reading** | No | Limited | Yes (hundreds/sec) |

## 5. Trending Data Points

### Walmart RFID Mandate

| Timeline | Requirement | Scope |
|----------|------------|-------|
| **Sept 2022** | Initial mandate announced | Home goods, sporting goods, electronics, toys |
| **Jan 2023** | Phase 1 enforcement | Suppliers must apply EPC-enabled UHF RFID tags |
| **2024** | Expanded categories | Automotive, home improvement, food/consumables (pilot) |
| **2025** | Further expansion | Top-stock and backroom visibility across most GM categories |
| **2026 (projected)** | Grocery categories | Fresh food visibility (pilot/rollout) |
| **Mandate details** | EPC/RAIN UHF RFID tags on each sellable unit | Compliant with GS1 SGTIN encoding |
| **Estimated tag volume** | Billions of tags annually | One of the world's largest RFID deployments |

**Impact:** Walmart's mandate is the single largest driver of UHF RFID tag adoption in retail, forcing thousands of suppliers to adopt item-level RFID tagging.

**Source URLs:**
- Walmart RFID requirements: https://corporate.walmart.com/
- RAIN Alliance coverage: https://www.rainrfid.org/

### EU Digital Product Passport (DPP)

| Timeline | Requirement | Scope |
|----------|------------|-------|
| **2024** | ESPR (Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation) adopted | Framework regulation |
| **2025** | Batteries Regulation DPP | Electric vehicle batteries, industrial batteries |
| **2026** | Textiles DPP (expected) | Apparel, footwear, textiles |
| **2027-2028** | Electronics, construction products | Phased rollout |
| **2030** | Most product categories | Broad coverage across EU market |
| **Technology** | QR codes + NFC/RFID recommended | Machine-readable, consumer-scannable |
| **Data required** | Materials, carbon footprint, repairability, recyclability | Stored in decentralized data carriers |

**Impact for RFID industry:** The EU DPP is expected to create massive demand for NFC tags (particularly NTAG 424 DNA for authentication) embedded in products, especially in fashion/textiles and electronics.

**Source URLs:**
- European Commission ESPR: https://commission.europa.eu/energy-climate-change-environment/standards-tools-and-labels/products-labelling-rules-and-requirements/sustainable-products/ecodesign-sustainable-products-regulation_en
- GS1 Digital Product Passport: https://www.gs1.org/standards/digital-product-passport
