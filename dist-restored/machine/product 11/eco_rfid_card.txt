## Description
Eco RFID cards in wood, PLA and paper materials for sustainable hotel, membership, access and branded credential programs.

## Summary
- Eco RFID cards use materials such as wood, PLA and paper to give hospitality, membership and branding programs a more sustainable credential option.
- The eco RFID cards are typically produced using biodegradable material, such as wood, PLA, paper etc., which help reduce plastic waste.
- Despite being eco-friendly, these cards are designed to be durable and long-lasting, ensuring they can withstand everyday use.
