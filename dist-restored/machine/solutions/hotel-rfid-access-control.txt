## FAQ
- Q: When should a hotel keep cards instead of switching to wristbands?
  A: Card-first programs still make sense for most room-key deployments, especially where guests expect a familiar form factor and amenity access is limited.
- Q: Can hotels run cards and wristbands together?
  A: Yes. Mixed programs are common when guest rooms use cards while pools, gyms or VIP amenities use wristbands.
- Q: What speeds up a hotel RFID quote?
  A: Sharing the current lock estate, chip requirement, preferred credential format and target sample quantity removes most early back-and-forth.
