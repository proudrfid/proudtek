## Description
FeliCa smart cards for high-speed transit, campus, loyalty and secure access applications, built around Sony's 13.56 MHz contactless platform.

## Summary
- FeliCa cards are high-speed contactless smart cards used for transit, campus, payment and secure access programs, especially where Sony's FeliCa ecosystem is already specified.
- FeliCa is designed for quick transactions, making it suitable for environments where high-speed processing is needed, like public transit systems or retail payments.
- FeliCa card supports strong encryption, making it secure for applications involving sensitive data, such as payments or personal identification.
