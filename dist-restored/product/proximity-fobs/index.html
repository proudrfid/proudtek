Technical Specifications

Chip OptionsEM4100 (read-only), T5577 (read/write), HID-compatible 125 kHz
Operating Frequency125 kHz (LF)
ProtocolEM modulation, HID ProxII, Indala (chip-dependent)
HousingABS plastic, drop-resistant
DimensionsTypical: 40 × 32 × 5 mm or Ø 35 mm disc with keyring hole
Color OptionsBlack, blue, red, green, grey, custom Pantone matching
Read Range3–8 cm
Key RingIntegrated stainless steel split ring or lanyard hole
Operating Temperature−20 °C to +55 °C
Data Retention> 10 years
Compatibility: EM4100 fobs work with EM-Marine readers; HID-compatible fobs work with HID ProxPoint Plus, ProxPro, and compatible third-party 125 kHz readers.

Applications

  • Residential and commercial building access
  • Gym and fitness center membership
  • Parking garage access
  • Gate and barrier control

Buyer Notes

  1. 1EM4100 keyfobs are the most cost-effective option for basic 125 kHz access; HID-compatible fobs work with existing HID ProxPoint readers.
  2. 2T5577 keyfobs can be field-cloned to match any 125 kHz credential — popular with locksmiths and property managers.
  3. 3ABS housing is durable for daily keychain carry; specify drop-test requirements if used in industrial environments.
  4. 4Custom color coding helps tenants and staff distinguish between different access zones or buildings.
