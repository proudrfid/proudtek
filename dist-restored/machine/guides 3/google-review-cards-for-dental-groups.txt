## FAQ
- Q: Should dental groups standardize one review-card format across every clinic?
  A: Often they can standardize the visual system, but the routing and exact prompt placement may still need to adapt to how each practice handles reception and checkout.
- Q: What should a dental-group rollout prove first?
  A: It should prove the branch routing, front-desk handoff and patient response before the group commits to a full multi-practice print batch.
