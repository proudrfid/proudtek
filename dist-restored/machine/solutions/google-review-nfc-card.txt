## FAQ
- Q: Do customers need an app to use a Google review NFC card?
  A: No. Any NFC-enabled iPhone (iPhone 7 or later running iOS 14+) or Android phone can tap the card and open the Google review page directly in the browser. No app download is required. The NTAG213 chip used in Proud Tek cards is compatible with all mainstream NFC-enabled smartphones.
- Q: How do I get my Google Business Profile review link?
  A: Log into Google Business Profile, select your location, click 'Ask for reviews' and copy the short review link. This is the URL that gets programmed into the NFC chip. Make sure the link opens correctly on a mobile browser before sending it for card production.
- Q: Can I update the NFC URL after the cards are printed?
  A: Yes. Proud Tek cards use an NTAG213 chip with a rewritable URL by default. You can reprogram the destination using any NFC-capable phone and a free app such as NFC Tools. If you prefer cards to be locked permanently to a single URL, this is also available — specify at order.
- Q: What is the minimum order quantity for Google review NFC cards?
  A: Sample orders start from 10–25 cards for design validation. Production runs typically start from 50–100 cards for single-location businesses. Multi-location orders with location-specific encoding start from 25 cards per location.
- Q: Are NFC review cards better than QR review stands?
  A: NFC cards produce faster action at the point of service because the tap is a single gesture. QR stands work better in environments where customers are seated and have time to frame and scan. The placement guide covers the conditions where each format performs best — many businesses use both.
- Q: How do multi-location businesses manage different review links?
  A: Each location's cards are encoded to that location's individual Google Business Profile review link during production. Proud Tek handles per-location encoding from a spreadsheet of review links — you do not need to handle individual card programming. Cards are shipped pre-sorted by location.
- Q: What card design works best for review prompts?
  A: Cards with a clear call to action ('Tap to leave a review'), a recognisable Google logo or star graphic, and brand colours consistently outperform generic white or minimal designs. The design and copy guide covers the specific elements that affect tap rate and customer confidence.
