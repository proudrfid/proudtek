## FAQ
- Q: What helps a VingCard compatibility request move fastest?
  A: The fastest requests usually include a current guest card sample or a clear VingCard lock or encoder reference plus notes on chip, encoding and whether the property wants plain or printed cards.
- Q: Should VingCard projects start with premium card materials?
  A: Usually no. It is safer to prove the baseline compatible card path first, then expand into premium materials once the estate and issue flow are already validated.
