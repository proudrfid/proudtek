## FAQ
- Q: Do hotel groups need different review cards for every property?
  A: Not always. Some groups can use shared print with editable routing, but mixed brand tiers or property types often need more location-specific handling than teams expect.
- Q: What should a hotel-group pilot prove first?
  A: It should prove property-level routing, front-desk fit and whether the guest prompt can be repeated consistently before the group prints for several hotels at once.
