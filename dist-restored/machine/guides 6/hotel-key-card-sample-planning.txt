- The best sample set answers one decision at a time instead of covering every possible card option.

## FAQ
- Q: How many hotel key card samples should a first round include?
  A: Usually only a control sample plus a small number of realistic alternatives. Too many variants often slow the project without creating a clearer decision.
- Q: What speeds up a hotel sample request most?
  A: A current card sample or clear lock reference, together with notes on encoding, material preference, print needs and rollout timing, usually produces the best first shortlist.
