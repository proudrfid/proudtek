## Description
Combi RFID cards with dual-frequency or multi-chip construction for migration projects, multi-application credentials and mixed-reader environments.

## Summary
- Combi RFID cards combine two credential technologies in one card, making them useful for phased migration, mixed-reader estates and multi-purpose access or attendance programs.
- Combi Cards often combine proximity or smartcard technology with long-range UHF (Ultra-High Frequency) technology. We have consider the interference between different chips, and make them work together perfectly.
- Although it embedded with two or three technology, the space occupied remains the same as regular single chip cards.
