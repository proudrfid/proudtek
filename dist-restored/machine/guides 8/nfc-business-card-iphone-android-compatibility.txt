## FAQ
- Q: Do NFC business cards need a QR fallback?
  A: In most real rollouts, yes. A QR fallback keeps the handoff usable when a phone is not tapped correctly, NFC is disabled or the user simply prefers to scan.
- Q: What should be tested before a larger NFC business card order?
  A: The team should test the live tap flow, iPhone and Android behavior, the landing-page experience and the QR fallback in the real networking moment before scaling the order.
