## FAQ
- Q: Should hotels begin with wood or PLA if sustainability is the goal?
  A: Only after the baseline compatible card path is already clear. The safer route is to prove compatibility first, then test whether wood or PLA adds enough brand value.
- Q: Is PVC still a reasonable hotel card choice?
  A: Yes. PVC remains the practical baseline in many hospitality rollouts, especially where scale, cost control and consistent handling matter most.
