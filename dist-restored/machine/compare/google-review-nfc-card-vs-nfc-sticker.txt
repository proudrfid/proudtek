## FAQ
- Q: Is an NFC review card always better than a sticker?
  A: No. Cards work well in guided staff interactions, but stickers can outperform them when the review prompt needs to live on a fixed surface that customers naturally notice.
- Q: What should a first format test measure?
  A: The first test should measure actual customer use, phone behavior, placement quality and review completion rather than only internal preference for one format.
