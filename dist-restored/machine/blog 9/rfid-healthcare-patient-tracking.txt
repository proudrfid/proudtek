## FAQ
- Q: Is patient data stored on the RFID wristband?
  A: No. Best practice is to store only a unique patient identifier (MRN or encounter number) on the wristband. All protected health information remains in the EHR system. The RFID tag serves as a link to the electronic record, not a data repository.
- Q: Can RFID interfere with medical equipment?
  A: Modern RFID readers designed for healthcare comply with IEC 60601-1-2 electromagnetic compatibility standards. HF readers operating at 13.56 MHz at typical power levels pose negligible interference risk. UHF readers should be tested in the specific clinical environment before permanent installation near sensitive diagnostic equipment.
- Q: How are RFID wristbands cleaned and disinfected?
  A: Silicone RFID wristbands withstand standard hospital disinfection protocols including wiping with alcohol-based sanitizers, chlorhexidine solutions and quaternary ammonium compounds. Some models are autoclavable. Disposable RFID wristbands are single-use and discarded with biohazard waste.
