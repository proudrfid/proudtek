- Start with the installed lock or encoder environment, not the card finish.

## FAQ
- Q: Do compatibility pages guarantee card support?
  A: No. They organize the first compatibility checks so the sample round starts from the most realistic card paths instead of broad catalog browsing.
- Q: What speeds up a compatibility reply most?
  A: A current guest card sample, a lock or encoder reference and a clear note on whether the property needs plain stock, branded cards or pre-encoding.
