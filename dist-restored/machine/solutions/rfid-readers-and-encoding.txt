## FAQ
- Q: What should decide the first RFID reader shortlist?
  A: The first shortlist should come from the exact card or tag family, host environment and integration workflow the team needs to support rather than from the reader brand or enclosure style alone.
- Q: Why do reader projects stall so often after purchase?
  A: They often stall because teams choose hardware before defining the software environment, the real credential set and the operating workflow the reader must support.
