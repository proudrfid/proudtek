## FAQ
- Q: How do I know which chip my hotel locks use?
  A: The fastest method is to send a photo of your current guest card or the lock brand and model. Most lock brands map cleanly to one or two chip families. We can confirm compatibility within one working day in most cases.
- Q: Can I order cards without providing key data?
  A: Yes, if you choose blank cards for front-desk encoding. Pre-encoded cards require the sector layout and key data, shared under a standard NDA. Most properties with existing encoder setups prefer the blank-card route.
- Q: Do hotel key cards need a magstripe co-layer?
  A: Only if your property has magnetic-stripe readers still in service — typically on older parking barriers, gym lockers or legacy room locks. Dual-interface cards with both RFID and magstripe are a standard catalogue option.
- Q: How long does hotel key card sampling take?
  A: Standard compatibility samples take 7–10 working days from confirmed chip and format. Artwork proofs add 2–3 working days. Providing a current card reference or lock model on first contact usually shortens this to the lower end of the range.
- Q: Can I get eco or wood hotel key cards in MIFARE Plus?
  A: Yes. Eco PLA and wood-veneer card constructions are available with MIFARE Classic, MIFARE Plus EV2 and DESFire EV3 inlays. Lead times for specialty materials are slightly longer than standard PVC.
- Q: What is the minimum order quantity for hotel key cards?
  A: Sample quantities start from 50–100 cards depending on the construction. Full production orders typically start from 500 cards for standard PVC and 200–300 for premium or eco materials. Contact the team for quantity-specific pricing.
- Q: What if my property uses multiple lock brands?
  A: Multi-brand estates are common and usually require a compatibility review across all lock platforms before a single chip family is confirmed. In some cases a dual-application or MIFARE Plus migration strategy can reduce the number of card SKUs in circulation.
