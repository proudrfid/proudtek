## Design Context

### Users
International business buyers and project managers evaluating custom RFID cards, laundry tags, wristbands, and related RFID products from a Chinese manufacturer. They need to understand product options, manufacturing capability, and supplier credibility quickly enough to decide whether to start a quote or custom-order conversation.

### Brand Personality
Reliable, industrial, professional. The experience should feel trustworthy, capable, and straightforward for B2B sourcing rather than trendy, playful, or consumer-retail oriented.

### Aesthetic Direction
Industrial B2B clarity with strong manufacturing credibility cues. Prioritize legibility, technical confidence, and frictionless inquiry paths. The site should support product evaluation and quotation intent without feeling generic, cluttered, or improvised.

### Design Principles
1. Make credibility visible early through structure, proof, and manufacturing clarity.
2. Keep quotation and inquiry actions obvious on every high-intent page.
3. Reduce noise so buyers can compare products, capabilities, and next steps quickly.
4. Favor professional restraint over decorative effects or consumer-style merchandising.
5. Use hierarchy and copy to support decision-making for international procurement audiences.
