## FAQ
- Q: Is UHF automatically better for asset tracking because the range is longer?
  A: No. UHF is attractive when longer-range or multi-item reads matter, but HF can still be the better fit when the workflow wants tighter user-controlled scans or denser item environments.
- Q: What should be tested before choosing HF or UHF?
  A: The most useful tests cover the real environment, item density, placement options, read distance and the software flow the operators will actually use.
