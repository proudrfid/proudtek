## Three constraints that follow from the scope line

1. **Scope is sales, not production.** All three certificates read "sales
   service" / "销售". Nothing on this site may describe them as production,
   plant, factory or manufacturing-scope certification. Removed on 2026-08-11:
   "Production ISO 9001:2015 certified", "our ISO 9001-certified Shenzhen
   facility", and — on two landing pages — "they are issued to the
   manufacturing entity, not a trading name", which the scope line
   contradicts outright.
2. **One address, and it is an office.** Registered address = audit address =
   a single unit in a technology building. No factory address appears on any
   of the three certificates. Manufacturing-site claims (two Shenzhen sites,
   ten lines, 305+ machines) are company statements and are now labelled as
   such on `/about/factory/`; they need the production entity's own
   certificates to become verifiable.
3. **Initial certification 2026-06-10.** These are new certificates. They do
   not evidence a long-running quality system, and no page should imply the
   company has been ISO 9001 certified since 2008 or for "18 years".

### Deliberately left alone

References to IATF 16949, AS9100D, ISO 13485, GMP, FSC, HACCP, NAS 412, FAA
AC 120-72B and similar in `industries/`, `solutions/`, `blog/`, `guides/`,
`compare/`, and in product pages' "regulatory anchors" / "qualification
standards" blocks, describe the **buyer's** compliance environment and what
the tag helps them satisfy. Those are accurate and useful; they are not
claims that Proud Tek holds the certificate.

The test to apply when editing: *"this tag helps you pass your AS9100D
audit"* is fine. *"our AS9100D QMS"* is not.

### Material-level vs company-level

`rfid-leather-keyfob` ("OEKO-TEX Leather Standard cleared") and similar
material claims describe a tested article or a certified input material,
which is a different claim from the company holding a certificate. Left in
place — but each needs a test report or the material supplier's certificate
on file to survive a buyer challenge. Worth a separate pass.
