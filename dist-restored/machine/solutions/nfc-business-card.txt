## FAQ
- Q: Do NFC business cards work on iPhones without an app?
  A: Yes. iPhone 7 and later running iOS 14 or above support background NFC tag reading — the review or contact page opens automatically when the card is tapped near the top of the phone. No app is required. iPhone XS and later have the widest NFC read range. Older iPhones (6s and below) do not support NFC tag reading.
- Q: Can I change the URL after the cards are printed?
  A: Yes, if your cards use a rewritable NTAG chip. Proud Tek encodes cards as rewritable by default. You can update the destination URL using any NFC-capable phone and a free app like NFC Tools. If you prefer cards permanently locked to a specific URL, this can be specified at order.
- Q: What is the cost per card for a team rollout?
  A: Standard PVC NFC business cards typically cost $2–5 USD per card at quantities of 50–200. Metal cards range from $10–20 USD. Pricing depends on material, finish, chip type and whether per-card URL personalisation is required. Contact the team for a quantity-specific quote.
- Q: Can each team member's card open a different URL?
  A: Yes. Proud Tek supports per-card URL encoding at the production stage for team rollouts. Provide a spreadsheet with each person's name and destination URL — cards are produced, encoded and optionally personalised in one production run.
- Q: What should the card tap open?
  A: The most common destinations are a LinkedIn profile, a vCard contact file (which adds the contact directly to the phone's address book), a personal landing page with contact details and portfolio, or a company website with UTM tracking. Landing pages with a clear call to action and mobile-optimised layout convert better than generic homepages.
- Q: What is the minimum order for NFC business cards?
  A: Sample orders start from 10 cards for design and tap validation. Team rollouts typically start from 50 cards. There is no upper limit — large team programs with 500+ cards are handled with the same production process.
- Q: Do metal NFC cards work on iPhones?
  A: Yes, if the inlay antenna is positioned correctly for iPhone's NFC reader location. Proud Tek's metal card design places the inlay at a position validated for iPhone 7 through iPhone 15. Request a test card if you need to validate a specific iPhone model before placing a full order.
