## FAQ
- Q: What should be most visible on a review card design?
  A: The main action should be most visible, whether that is a tap, a scan or a short cue that tells the customer what to do next within a few seconds.
- Q: Should review cards include both NFC and QR?
  A: Often yes, but the design still needs one obvious lead action so the customer is not confused by competing cues or too much copy.
