## FAQ
- Q: What does your warranty cover?
  A: Proud RFID offer one year warranty for all the product we supplied.
- Q: How do I file a warranty claim?
  A: Please write email, or contact by instant chatting tools, or call us.
- Q: How About The Return Policy?
  A: If the product has quality problem, we will tell client to return the goods.
- Q: Does Free Shipping Include Returns As Well?
  A: Yes, for the return product which quality problem, it will be free shipping.
- Q: Can I have a sample order?
  A: After price confirmation, you can require for samples to check our quality, for the sample which we have stock and doesn’t further processing, we would send you free sample, as long as you afford the courier freight.If the sample needs to be produced or customized, we will charge $30-$100 to cover the production or development cost.
- Q: How long can I expect to get the sample?
  A: For the ones which we have stock, the sample normally be shipped within 1 or 2 days after customer confirmation. For other, it would be dependent on the production and development time.
- Q: Can I buy small quantity to check the product quality?
  A: Yes, we welcome customer to place test order before mass production.
- Q: Do you have any MOQ limit?
  A: It depends on the inventory status, for the product which we have stock, any quantity would be welcome.
- Q: What about the lead time for mass production?
  A: It depends on the order quantity and the season you place the order. We suggest that you start inquiry two months before the date
- Q: Can we get support if we have our own market position?
  A: Please inform us your detailed mind on your market demand, we will discuss and propose helpful suggestion for you, to find the best solution for you.
