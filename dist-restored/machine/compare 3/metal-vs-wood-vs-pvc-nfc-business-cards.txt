## FAQ
- Q: Is metal the best option for all premium NFC card projects?
  A: No. Metal fits some premium programs well, but wood or even carefully finished PVC can be better if the brand story or rollout model points elsewhere.
- Q: Should teams decide on material before workflow?
  A: No. The tap behavior, target phones and redirect logic should be confirmed first so the material decision supports the actual use case.
