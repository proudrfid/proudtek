## 4. People and authorship (settles D-06)

rfidak.com names **no individuals**. Functions only: "International Sales — quotes in 24–48 hours", "RF / Production Engineering — chip & antenna matching", "Quality Control — 100% read-test before shipment". Articles are bylined "RFIDAK RFID Editorial Team — manufacturer editorial team" with an author profile page. Adopted for proudtek.com: organisation/role authorship (Proud Tek Editorial Team; reviewed by Proud Tek RF / Production Engineering or Quality Control by topic); named individuals removed from bylines, JSON-LD, mirrors, llms.txt, review-board and leadership pages.

## 5. Product scope (settles D-07)

rfidak's "always in stock" chip list includes **Hitag 1/2/S** (LF); **FeliCa (Sony) is not listed** anywhere. Therefore: `/product/hitag-2-card/` and `/ja/製品/ヒタグ2カード/` keep redirecting to `/products/rfid-cards/` (HITAG is a live chip family without a proudtek product page — creating one is new content, out of scope); `/product/felica-card/` keeps redirecting to `/products/rfid-cards/` (not offered). No rule change.
