## Strategic Summary

### Keyword Distribution by Intent
| Intent Type | Count | Focus |
|-------------|-------|-------|
| Transactional | 52 | Product pages, landing pages — direct conversion targets |
| Commercial | 95 | Product and landing pages — buyers in evaluation/comparison phase |
| Informational | 53 | Blog posts, guides, comparison pages — top-of-funnel traffic and authority building |

### Priority Implementation Order
1. **Immediate (Month 1-2):** Buyer-intent keywords (#121-150) — these have the highest conversion rates and directly generate leads/orders
2. **High Priority (Month 2-3):** Product keywords (#1-50) — build out category and product page optimization
3. **Medium Priority (Month 3-4):** Industry vertical keywords (#151-175) — create industry-specific landing pages
4. **Ongoing (Month 4-6):** Application keywords (#51-90) and technology keywords (#91-120) — content marketing and technical authority
5. **Continuous (Ongoing):** Trending keywords (#191-200) — publish timely content to capture emerging demand before competitors
6. **Supporting (Ongoing):** Comparison keywords (#176-190) — mid-funnel content that builds trust and captures decision-stage traffic

### Content Type Requirements
| Page Type | Keyword Count | Purpose |
|-----------|--------------|---------|
| Product page | 62 | Direct product matching, catalog SEO |
| Landing page | 53 | Buyer intent capture, supplier/manufacturer queries |
| Blog post | 35 | Informational content, long-tail traffic, topical authority |
| Guide | 16 | Technical depth, specification research, developer audience |
| Comparison page | 15 | Decision-stage content, chip/technology evaluation |
| Product category page | 9 | Category-level navigation, head terms |
| Industry landing page | 13 | Vertical-specific solutions, industry authority |
| FAQ / landing page | 3 | Buyer logistics questions (MOQ, lead time, samples) |

### Key Observations for Proud Tek
- **Digital Product Passport (DPP)** keywords are the single largest emerging opportunity — EU regulations will make RFID/NFC mandatory for multiple product categories starting 2027, and search volume is growing month over month
