## FAQ
- Q: What matters most for a tabletop review prompt?
  A: Placement and clarity usually matter most, because the prompt often has to work without staff explanation and must attract attention on its own.
- Q: Should a tabletop prompt always be a rigid card?
  A: Not always. A rigid card can feel more premium, but a sticker or integrated stand can be better when the surface, cleaning routine or available space changes the practical fit.
