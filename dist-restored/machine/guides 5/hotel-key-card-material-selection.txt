## FAQ
- Q: Should hotels start with premium materials or standard PVC?
  A: Most projects move faster when they validate a standard compatible card first, then compare upgraded materials only once the property understands the technical and operational baseline.
- Q: What usually decides a hotel card material choice most?
  A: The decision usually comes down to guest experience goals, replacement behavior, print expectations, compatibility confidence and whether the property truly benefits from a stronger eco or premium story.
