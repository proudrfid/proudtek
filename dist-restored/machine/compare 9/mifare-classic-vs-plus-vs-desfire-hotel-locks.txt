## FAQ
- Q: Is DESFire automatically the right choice for hotel locks?
  A: Not automatically. DESFire often suits higher-security programs well, but the current lock estate and migration pressure can still make MIFARE Plus the more practical path.
- Q: Should hotels choose the chip family before checking encoder compatibility?
  A: No. Encoder and lock compatibility should be part of the first chip-family decision because they shape which migration paths are realistic.
