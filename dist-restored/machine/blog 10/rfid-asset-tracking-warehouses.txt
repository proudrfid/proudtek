## FAQ
- Q: How many portal readers do I need for a warehouse?
  A: The number depends on your facility layout and tracking requirements. At minimum, install portals at every dock door (receiving and shipping) and at transitions between major zones. A 50,000 square foot warehouse with 4 dock doors and 3 internal zones typically needs 6-8 portal read points. A professional site survey identifies the optimal locations and antenna configurations.
- Q: Does RFID work on metal assets and containers?
  A: Standard UHF label tags perform poorly on metal because the metal surface detunes the tag antenna. On-metal tags use a spacer layer (foam or ceramic) between the tag and the metal surface to maintain performance. These tags cost $0.50-$3.00 more than standard labels but deliver reliable read ranges of 2-6 meters on metallic assets.
- Q: What is the read accuracy of a warehouse RFID portal?
  A: A properly installed and tuned dock-door portal achieves 99.5-99.9 percent read accuracy for tagged items passing through the read zone. Accuracy depends on tag quality, antenna placement, reader power settings and tag orientation diversity. Items with tags facing away from all antennas or shielded by liquid or metal may require additional antenna positions or tag placement guidelines.
- Q: Can RFID replace barcodes in my warehouse?
  A: RFID can replace barcodes for most tracking functions, but many operations maintain both. RFID excels at bulk reading, automated portals and visual search. Barcodes remain useful for point-of-use verification, label readability by humans and integration with systems that expect barcode data. - Q: How do I tag existing inventory for RFID tracking?
